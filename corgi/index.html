Foundational knowledge

What is an ETF?

An ETF is a regulated fund that holds a portfolio (stocks, bonds, commodities, crypto, etc.) and issues shares listed on exchanges. Corgi is the issuer; investors hold fund shares, not direct ownership of every underlying stock.

Two markets — primary vs secondary

ETF activity splits into two markets. Secondary is investors buying and selling existing ETF shares on exchanges — this is the vast majority of volume. Primary is Authorized Participants (APs) creating or redeeming shares directly with the fund — this changes shares outstanding and AUM, but happens far less often.

Data sourcing

Fund accounting requires five consolidated input datasets. Vendor redundancy (2–3 sources per market-data pipeline) provides failover and cross-check — not because two vendor feeds alone are sufficient. Holdings quantity, cash balances, shares outstanding, and trade history come from operational feeds; trial balance and NAV lines are computed from these inputs.

Vendor · Operational · Computed · Manual — GL sub-categories used throughout: BASE (cash/settlement), ETF (primary market flows), EQ (equity), FI (fixed income), ST (short-term).

Product-specific add-ons outside the five: derivatives marks (2×/buffer funds), crypto spot (Kraken · Coinbase), index/benchmark levels (buffer funds). Internal fund master and fee schedules are configuration, not vendor pipelines.

Procurement paths

Direct enterprise feeds — required to own the system of record. Bloomberg Data License, LSEG/Refinitiv, and ICE are custom-quoted; typical range ~$50K–$150K/year at ETF-issuer scale for pricing + corp actions + reference. Redundancy adds cost for failover, not 3× data volume.
Administrator pass-through — published per-security rates when market data is sourced through the fund administrator. Source: U.S. Bank fund servicing fee schedule (SEC filing, 2025). Thematic equity fund (~100 holdings): ~$5K–$6K/year market data. Fixed-income fund (~200 bonds): ~$28K–$40K/year — FI pricing dominates.

Detail by datapoint

All ETFs — Cash

Settled and unsettled cash balances across all currencies, converted to fund base currency for the trial balance.

Source: Dataset 4 — Operational truth.

All ETFs — Investment receivable / payable

Amounts due from sales not yet settled (receivable) or owed on purchases not yet settled (payable). These bridge trade date and settlement date.

Source: Computed · Dataset 4 — Operational truth.

All ETFs — Primary market (subscriptions / redemptions)

Pending AP creates (receivable), pending redeems (payable), and settled capital flow. Cash owed to the fund from creations not yet settled, or cash owed on pending redemptions.

Source: Dataset 4 — Operational truth.

Equity ETF — Cost of investments

Original purchase price of equity holdings, maintained per tax lot or aggregate, adjusted for stock splits, spin-offs, and mergers.

Source: Computed · Dataset 4 — Operational truth · Dataset 2 — Corporate actions & FI events.

Equity ETF — Net unrealized appreciation/depreciation

Difference between current fair value of equity holdings and their cost basis. Positive = unrealized gain; negative = unrealized loss.

Source: Computed · Dataset 1 — Pricing & FX · Dataset 3 — Security reference · Dataset 4 — Operational truth.

Equity ETF — Dividends receivable & dividend income

Receivable: dividend declared but not yet paid. Income: recognized when the fund is entitled to the dividend (typically ex-date).

Source: Computed · Dataset 2 — Corporate actions & FI events · Dataset 4 — Operational truth. Withholding rates from Dataset 3 — Security reference.

Bond ETF — Interest, amortization & accretion

Coupon accrual, interest income, premium amortization, and discount accretion on bond holdings.

Source: Computed · Dataset 3 — Security reference · Dataset 4 — Operational truth (accrual); Dataset 1 — Pricing & FX · Dataset 3 — Security reference · Dataset 4 — Operational truth (amort/accret).

Equity ETF / Bond ETF — Realized gain/loss

Profit or loss recognized when a security is sold. Short-term if holding period < 1 year.

Source: Computed · Dataset 4 — Operational truth.

All ETFs — Fees, tax & settlement

Unitary fee accrual, dividend withholding tax, and settlement breakage.

Source: Computed · Dataset 4 — Operational truth (fees, breakage); Dataset 2 — Corporate actions & FI events · Dataset 3 — Security reference · Dataset 4 — Operational truth (withholding).

All ETFs — NAV rollups

Total assets, total liabilities, net assets, NAV per share, net income, total capital.

Source: Computed · Dataset 1 — Pricing & FX · Dataset 2 — Corporate actions & FI events · Dataset 3 — Security reference · Dataset 4 — Operational truth. Shares outstanding from Dataset 4 — Operational truth.
